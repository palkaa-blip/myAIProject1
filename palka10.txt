 AI THEORY-              2nd oct

 Introduction to AI?-
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions.

Types of AI-

Narrow AI: Does one specific task well (alexa, voice assistants). 
General AI (AGI): A hypothetical system with human-level ability across any task. It doesn't exist yet(it perform multiple tasks).

Everyday examples: Netflix recommendations, Google Maps traffic predictions, Siri/Alexa, fraud detection in banking, and chatbots.
 
what is  Generative AI?-
Generative AI is a type of AI that creates new content, such as text, images, audio, video, or code, instead of just analyzing or classifying existing data.

How it differs from traditional AI

Traditional AI: Answers questions like "Is this email spam?" or "Is this a cat or a dog?" It labels or predicts.
Generative AI: Produces something new, like "Write an email," "Draw a cat in a spacesuit," or "Compose a melody."


Traditional AI/ML looks at data and tells you something about it.
Generative AI looks at data and makes something new.

Simple examples
Traditional ML: "Is this email spam?" → Yes / No
Traditional ML: "What will this house sell for?" → ₹50 lakh
Generative AI: "Write me an email." → A brand new email
Generative AI: "Draw a cat on the moon." → A brand new image
 

what is AI agents, chatbots, GenAI tools?-
GenAI tool: You give it an input, it creates something. (Midjourney makes an image, Copilot suggests code.)

Chatbot: You talk to it, and it answers. (ChatGPT, Claude, a bank's support bot.)

AI agent: You give it a goal, and it does the work using tools. (Searches, books, emails, runs code.)

Easy analogy

GenAI tool = a camera: you use it, it produces something.
Chatbot = a help desk person: you ask, they reply.
AI agent = a personal assistant: you say "organize my trip," and they go do it.

what is LLM- LLM stands for Large Language Model. It is an AI model trained on huge amounts of text (books, websites, articles, code) so it can understand and generate human language.
